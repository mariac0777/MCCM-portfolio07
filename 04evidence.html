THE APPROACH

Building a Structured Social Media Workflow

Effective social media support requires more than creating individual posts. It involves understanding the brand, identifying its audience, planning relevant content and maintaining an organized workflow.

For this case study, I developed a complete conceptual social media workflow for MANTIZ — Coastal Hostel & Community, applying a structured approach to content planning, organization, community management and performance tracking.
